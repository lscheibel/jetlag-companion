# Tentacle Questions

**Format:** "Within ____ of me, which ____ are you nearest to? (You must also be within ____)"
**Answer:** the name of one of the listed locations, or a statement that they are out of range
**Time to answer:** 5 minutes
**Hider reward:** draw 4 cards, keep 2

## General rules

- **Not available in small games.**
- If the hider is inside the stated radius they must name the specific location they are nearest to. If they're outside it, they simply say they're not in range.
- Most valuable late in the game: they clear out dense areas fast, but at a very high card cost.
- Mental model: each qualifying location (all the zoos, say) becomes a tentacle reaching out from the seekers. One of them touches the hider and reports back.

---

## Medium games

All of these use whatever your mapping app correctly categorizes as such. Radius: **1 mile / 2 km**.

- **Museums**
- **Libraries**
- **Movie theaters**
- **Hospitals**

---

## Large games — add

Radius: **15 miles / 25 km**.

- **Metro lines** — these show up as colored lines in Google Maps.
- **Zoos**
- **Aquariums**
- **Amusement parks**
