# Seeker Questions — Overview

The six question categories available to seekers, with their answer formats, time limits and card costs.

| # | Category | Format | Answer | Time limit | Hider draws |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Matching](01-matching-questions.md) | "Is your nearest ____ the same as my ____?" | yes / no | 5 min | 3, keep 1 |
| 2 | [Measuring](02-measuring-questions.md) | "Compared to me, are you closer to or further from ____?" | closer / further | 5 min | 3, keep 1 |
| 3 | [Radar](03-radar-questions.md) | "Are you within ____ of me?" | yes / no | 5 min | 2, keep 1 |
| 4 | [Thermometer](04-thermometer-questions.md) | "After traveling ____, am I hotter or colder?" | hotter / colder | 5 min | 2, keep 1 |
| 5 | [Photo](05-photo-questions.md) | "Send me a photo of ____." | photo, or "I cannot answer" | 10 min (S/M), 20 min (L) | 1, keep 1 |
| 6 | [Tentacle](06-tentacle-questions.md) | "Within ____ of me, which ____ are you nearest to?" | a location name, or out of range | 5 min | 4, keep 2 |

**Metric values throughout are rounded to convenient numbers, not exact conversions.** Pick one system and stick to it for a whole game — mixing them changes the size of the radii.

| Imperial | Metric |
|---|---|
| ¼ mile | 500 m |
| ½ mile | 1 km |
| 1 mile | 2 km |
| 3 miles | 5 km |
| 5 miles | 10 km |
| 10 miles | 15 km |
| 15 miles | 25 km |
| 25 miles | 40 km |
| 50 miles | 80 km (radar) / 75 km (thermometer) |
| 100 miles | 160 km |
| 5 × 5 feet | 2 × 2 m |

## Cross-cutting rules

- **Map boundaries define reality.** Features outside the boundary don't exist. If no qualifying feature exists inside it, the question returns a null answer — which still counts as answered, and the hider still draws cards. (Applies to matching and measuring.)
- **Measure to the map icon** for point locations, even when that produces odd results.
- **Seekers resolve ambiguity.** When a category is fuzzy, the asking seekers must state what they mean.
- **Availability by game size:** tentacle questions are excluded from small games; thermometer, photo and tentacle questions unlock additional options at medium and large sizes.
