# Photo Questions

**Format:** "Send me a photo of ____."
**Answer:** a photo meeting every stated requirement, or "I cannot answer the question" when the subject doesn't exist anywhere in the hiding zone.
**Time to answer:** 10 minutes (small and medium games), 20 minutes (large games)
**Hider reward:** draw 1 card, keep 1

## General rules

- Every photo must be in the phone's normal aspect ratio.
- Seekers may **not** use Google Street View to analyze a photo or identify stations remotely.
- Photos help you get a rough read on the hider's surroundings, or confirm you've reached the right spot.

---

## Small games

### Any Building Visible from Transit Station
Shoot from directly outside a station entrance; with multiple entrances, pick whichever you want. The frame must show the roof and both sides of the building, with its top in the upper ⅓ of the shot.

### Widest Street
Both sides of the street must be in frame. Background is not required.

### Tree
The whole tree must be in frame.

### Tallest Structure in Your Current Sightline
Judged from your viewpoint, not by objective height — a nearby apartment block that looms larger than a distant skyscraper is the correct subject. Must show the top and both sides, top in the upper ⅓ of the frame.

### You
Selfie mode, phone held perpendicular to the ground, arm fully extended, default lens, no zoom.

### The Sky
Phone flat on the ground shooting straight up, default lens, no zoom.

## Medium and large games — add

### Tallest Building Visible from Transit Station
Again judged from your perspective rather than by absolute height. Stand directly outside a station entrance (your choice if there are several). Roof and both sides in frame, top in the upper ⅓.

The station building itself normally doesn't qualify. The exception is when an unrelated tall building sits on top of the station — e.g. the MetLife building above Grand Central would count.

### Trace Nearest Street/Path
The street or path has to be visible in your mapping app; trace it from one intersection to the next.

Two suggested techniques: screenshot the map and use your photo editor's drawing tools to black out everything except the street, or lay a sheet of paper over the phone and trace it by hand.

### 2 Buildings
Frame must include the bottom of the buildings and up to four stories.

### Restaurant Interior
No zoom, and the shot must be taken through the window from outside.

### Park
No zoom, phone perpendicular to the ground, standing at least 5 feet / 2 m clear of any obstruction.

### Grocery Store Aisle
No zoom. Stand at the end of the aisle and shoot straight down it.

### Place of Worship
Capture a 5' × 5' / 2 × 2 m area containing three distinct elements. Test for "distinct": someone standing at that spot could confidently match your photo and rule out anywhere else.

### Train Platform
Same requirement — a 5' × 5' / 2 × 2 m area with three distinct elements passing the same matching test.

---

## Large games — add

### ½ Mile / 1 km of Streets Traced
The trace must be continuous, contain 5 turns, and never double back. Send it oriented north–south. All streets must appear in the mapping app. (Same tracing techniques as above.)

### Tallest Mountain Visible from Transit Station
Tallest from your sightline, not absolute height — a nearby peak that looks bigger than a far-off giant is the right subject. Maximum 3× zoom; the summit must sit in the upper ⅓ of the frame.

### The Biggest Body of Water in Your Zone
Maximum 3× zoom. Frame must show either both shores or the horizon.
- Water visible from the zone but not touching it doesn't count.
- If a large body of water only clips the edge of the zone, it still counts as the biggest even when the portion inside the zone is smaller than another water body wholly inside it.

### 5 Buildings
Frame must include the bottom and up to four stories.
