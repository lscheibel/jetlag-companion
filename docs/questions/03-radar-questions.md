# Radar Questions

**Format:** "Are you within ____ of me?"
**Answer:** yes / no
**Time to answer:** 5 minutes
**Hider reward:** draw 2 cards, keep 1

## General rules

- Good for confirming you're in roughly the right area, or for wiping out a dense chunk of the map.
- Easiest way to measure: long-press or right-click in Google Maps and use the Measure tool.
- **A radar asks about the hider's location, not their hiding zone.** If the radius covers part of the hiding zone but not the spot the hider is actually standing at the moment of answering, the answer is no.
