# Measuring Questions

**Format:** "Compared to me, are you closer to or further from ____?"
**Answer:** closer / further
**Time to answer:** 5 minutes
**Hider reward:** draw 3 cards, keep 1

## General rules

- Broadly useful throughout the game, and good for cutting the map along unusual lines.
- Anything outside the map boundary doesn't exist for game purposes. If there is no qualifying feature inside the boundary, you get a **null answer** — it counts as an answered question and the hider still draws.
- Unless stated otherwise, distance to a place is measured from its **map icon**.

---

## Transit-Related

### Commercial Airport
If it's unclear, treat an airport as commercial when Google Flights shows flights to or from it.

### High-Speed Train Line
Use the locally applicable definition of high-speed rail where one exists. Otherwise fall back on the EU standard: roughly 250 km/h (155 mph) or more on purpose-built high-speed lines, and around 200 km/h (124 mph) on existing lines that have been upgraded for the purpose.

### Rail Station
Both light and heavy rail count, and metro/subway stations count too.

---

## Borders

### International Border
Enclaves count.

### 1st Administrative Division Border
A border between top-level divisions — US states, Swiss cantons, Japanese prefectures.

### 2nd Administrative Division Border
A border between the next level down — US counties, Swiss districts, Japanese subprefectures.

## Natural

### Sea Level
This is a question about your **altitude**. Your phone's compass app will give you a reading, but be warned that it is sometimes wrong — don't treat it as gospel.

### Body of Water
Any body of water that is named in your maps app. Swimming pools are excluded.

### Coastline
Where a coastline starts is a notoriously hard geographic question, so the rulebook uses a deliberately objective working definition: a coastline is any point where land meets the ocean, a great lake, or a body of water connected to one of those by a waterway that never narrows below one mile / 2 km across.

Don't lean on this for pinpoint precision — talk it through with each other if something looks odd.

### Mountain
Whatever your mapping app correctly classifies as a mountain. Measure from the map icon.

### Park
Whatever your mapping app correctly classifies as a park. Measure from the map icon.

Same quirk as in the matching version: standing a mile inside a huge park, you may have to report being a mile from any park, because the icon is that far away. Icon-measuring is kept anyway as the cleanest objective system.

---

## Places of Interest

All use your mapping app's categorization, measured from the map icon.

- **Amusement Park**
- **Zoo**
- **Aquarium**
- **Golf Course** — outdoor only; no mini golf, no driving ranges. Seekers should state what they count when asking.
- **Museum**
- **Movie Theater**

---

## Public Utilities

Same rule: mapping app categorization, measured from the map icon.

- **Hospital**
- **Library**
- **Foreign Consulate** — honorary consulates excluded.
