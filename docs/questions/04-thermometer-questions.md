# Thermometer Questions

**Format:** "After traveling ____, am I hotter or colder?"
**Answer:** hotter / colder
**Time to answer:** 5 minutes
**Hider reward:** draw 2 cards, keep 1

## How it works

1. Tell the hider you are beginning a thermometer and send your **starting location** (a dropped pin via Google or Apple Maps is recommended).
2. Travel at least the stated distance, measured **as the crow flies**.
3. Send your **new location**.
4. If the new position is nearer to the hider's current location than the starting position was, the answer is **hotter**; otherwise **colder**.

Best used to check whether you're heading in the right direction.

---

## Available distances

Metric values are rounded to convenient numbers, not exact conversions.

### Small games
- ½ mile / 1 km
- 3 miles / 5 km

### Medium and large games — add
- 10 miles / 15 km

### Large games — add
- 50 miles / 75 km
