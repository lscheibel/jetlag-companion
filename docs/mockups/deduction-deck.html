Every answer shrinks the map

The search area is a left fold over an ordered list of constraints, seeded with the valid hiding area. That one shape gives the seeker a live map, an editable list, an undo that is byte-identical, and a place for deductions the app could never make on its own. Around it sit the two things that make the game a game: a hand of cards and a curse in flight.

The fold

One operation, applied over and over

Each constraint is one operation on the previous snapshot, which is what makes the fold incremental, cacheable and reversible. Disabling the third constraint is not an undo feature — it is the same fold, run without that step.

A constraint is not owned by the question that produced it. It is a record with a source and an enabled flag — which is why a hand-drawn shape, a disabled constraint, a corrected answer and a bulk reset are one mechanism rather than four features.
Seeker deduction

What is left, and what took it away

One hider team at a time — each (seeker team, hider team) pairing has its own fold, so the map never mixes two searches together. The share eliminated is on screen, and so is how much the newest answer contributed.

The deck

Cards that nudge, and never block

The hand limit is a prompt, not a wall. A hider over the limit is asked to play or discard, ignores it if they like, and keeps playing — the app has no opinion it is willing to enforce.

Fold cache
Keyed by area-pack version plus the ordered constraint ids, so a disable-and-re-enable returns the previous geometry rather than recomputing to something slightly different.
Coordinates
WGS84 lng/lat everywhere. Metres enter only when building geometry from a distance, measuring, and choosing a snap tolerance — each at its own latitude.
Slivers
Long edges densified, geometry simplified and snapped between fold steps. The fifteenth answer is where skipping this starts to hurt.
Enforcement
None, anywhere on this deck. Hand limits, curse costs and hiding zones all warn and continue.
