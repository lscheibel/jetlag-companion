Zero Lag Question Loop
U Zero Lag · Hide + Seek companion · Deck 03 / 04 · Question loop

Ask anything. Answer in one tap

The loop the whole game runs on: a seeker team picks a question and a target, the hider gets a push and a countdown, and the app offers a suggestion it recomputes until the moment of submitting. The suggestion is never a verdict — override is always the same size as accept, and "not possible to answer" is a real answer that draws real cards.

Seeker side

Pick a question, pick who it goes to

One hider team by default. Only questions flagged multi-target in the deck builder can go to several at once, and each targeted team answers and draws independently. Card cost and reward are on the tile, before the tap, not after it.

Answer-time truth

The question is evaluated when it is answered

Not when it was asked, not against a committed zone, not against a team centroid — against the live position of the player who is answering, at the instant they submit. Which is why the suggestion keeps recomputing while they decide, and can visibly flip mid-decision.

Three times are stored: the server's receive time, the client's submit time for display, and the elapsed time the answering device measured. Device clocks are never compared to each other. Ordering is a per-game sequence number.
Hider side

One tap to accept, one tap to disagree

The suggestion shows its inputs and how fresh they are, so a hider can tell in a glance whether to trust it. Turn location off entirely and the flow is exactly as fast — the buttons are the same buttons, there is simply no suggestion above them.

Deadline
Displayed, never enforced. The ring runs out and the question stays answerable; the elapsed time is recorded either way.
Answer targets
Yes / No at 74 px tall, side by side, equal weight. Secondary answers keep 48 px.
Multi-target
One per-question flag, off by default. Each targeted team answers, draws and folds independently.
One definition
The suggestion runs the same constraint definition the seeker's map folds — evaluated as a point test instead of a geometry. They cannot drift apart.
