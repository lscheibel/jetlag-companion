U Zero Lag · Hide + Seek companion · Deck 06 · Seeker map

The map is the tool. Everything else gets out of the way

A seeker team lives on this screen for hours. Every pixel the app spends on its own furniture is a pixel of city somebody cannot see, so the chrome here is deliberately thin: five small controls down one edge, one line along the bottom, and a timer. Nothing is coloured until it is switched on.

Resting state

What it looks like when nobody is doing anything

Which hider you are hunting, how much of the map is gone, how long is left, and a way in to each of the four things a seeker does. That is the entire interface until somebody touches it.

The four tools

One rail, one job each

Deductions, measuring, pins and the zone you are about to search. They are the same size, the same weight and the same colour until one is active, because none of them is more important than the map underneath.

Chrome
Five 40 px controls, one bar and a timer. Everything translucent over blur, everything stroked rather than filled, all of it in ink until it is on.
Reach
The rail is bottom-right and the bar runs beside it, so both sit under a right thumb holding the phone at chest height. Nothing lives in the top corners.
Colour
The only saturated things on the map are the game: transit lines, team pins, the surviving area. No control is coloured unless it is doing something.
Tools take over
An active tool replaces the bottom bar instead of adding a second one, so the map never loses more than one strip of height.
